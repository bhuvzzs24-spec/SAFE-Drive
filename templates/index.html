What measurements do I need?

Use a fixed camera and at least four matching, non-collinear reference points on the road plane.

  • image_points_px: image [x, y] in pixels.
  • road_points_m: matching road [x, y] in metres, in the same order.

Measure real distances. Client checks validate the structure; the analysis worker checks the geometry. Neither establishes field accuracy.

Experimental inference. Human review required.

The optional adapter uses a four-class helmet detector and associates a head-region box only when its center falls in a tracked pedestrian's upper body region. Ambiguous associations are marked uncertain; no matching detection produces no class. There is no temporal confirmation or helmet violation event, and no compliance counts are inferred.
