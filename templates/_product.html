Compatible YOLO detections become confirmed, session-specific DeepSORT tracks. Inspect classes, observation history and the original image coordinates behind each measurement.

05 / Approach-risk screening

Look ahead.
Keep uncertainty in view.

Calibrated trajectories support time-to-collision and closest-approach screening under a locally constant velocity assumption. They surface situations for investigation, not predictions of an inevitable collision.

09 / Designed for responsible interpretation

Clarity includes
knowing the limits.

SAFE-Drive is research software. Detector quality, occlusion, camera geometry and timing affect every interpretation. Validate performance with representative footage and independent ground truth.

  • Approach-risk screening is experimental. Accident detection is not implemented.
  • Helmet labels require a separate compatible model and remain experimental.
  • No identity inference, automated penalties or determinations of fault.
  • Use footage you are permitted to process, and keep private evidence private.
